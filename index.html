Core Capabilities in v5.0

Native performance on Windows and Android without third-party relay servers or browser dashboards.

Rust Native Win32 GUI & Tray

Single lightweight Rust binary with a native Win32/GDI dark window, live QR pairing, 3-step Tailscale onboarding wizard, and System Tray close-to-background.

Per-User Tailscale Zero Re-Pairing

Connect over your own private tailnet. Fast boot polling, automatic endpoint sync, and parallel LAN-to-Tailscale failover eliminate QR re-scanning after sleep or network switches.

24/7 Android Companion Media Notification

A native Android Foreground Service keeps connection status live and puts interactive Prev, Play/Pause, and Next controls right in your notification shade and lock screen.

COM / WinRT Control & DPAPI Security

Direct Windows COM audio (Master, devices, and SteelSeries Sonar), WinRT media transport, hybrid WMI + OLED brightness, and DPAPI-protected SHA-256 TLS certificate pinning.

How to Connect

Three quick steps with zero terminal commands required.

STEP 01

Install on Windows

Run `PCRemoteRustSetup.exe` and open PC Remote. The native window displays your pairing QR code and 3-step Tailscale setup wizard.

STEP 02

Install Android Companion

Install `PCRemoteApp.apk` on your Android phone. For outside-home access, sign in to Tailscale on both your PC and phone.

STEP 03

Pair Once & Roam Freely

Scan the QR code once to pin your PC's TLS certificate. Smart roaming and the 24/7 companion notification handle every reconnect automatically.
